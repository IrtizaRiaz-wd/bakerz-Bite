PHILOSOPHY & HERITAGE

The Alchemy of Patience, Butter & Time

"We believe the pinnacle of luxury is not haste, but the patient mastery of micro-folds, cocoa crystals, and natural levain."

Founded with the conviction that patisserie is sculptural culinary architecture, Bakerz Bite combines classic French pastry discipline with rich botanical ingredients. Every single croissant is folded 72 times by hand over 72 hours of slow fermentation. Every mirror glaze entremet undergoes an 18-hour cold setting cycle so that when sliced, the textures transition seamlessly from brittle obsidian glaze to velvet mousse and hazelnut crunch.

100% Halal Couverture

Natural citrus pectin and vegetable agar-agar. Pure single-origin Valrhona cacao from Côte d’Ivoire and Madagascar.

AOP Normandy Butter

Exclusively 84% butterfat cultured butter imported directly from Normandy for authentic honeycomb crumb.
